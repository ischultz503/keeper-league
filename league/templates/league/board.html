How the projection fills empty picks
  • Best player available by ADP; players with no ADP rank go last.
  • Keepers, your locked calls and your sandbox picks are off the board — nobody drafts them.
  • A pick burned to pay for a keeper is skipped. Nobody moves up; the slot is simply dead.
  • No second QB or TE before Round 9, and no K or DEF until the last two rounds.
  • Nothing here is saved. It is recomputed every time you press the button, and the same inputs always produce the same board.
